# Soul

Manifesto, positioning, taste, refusals, the three EU gates, brand constraint, machinery, and how to talk to me. Read before any design, UX, or content work.

## The three gates

1. **WCAG 2.2 AA** - computed, never assumed. 2.1 AA / EN 301 549 is the legal floor, not the target.
2. **EU AI Act Article 50** - transparency, human oversight, synthetic content marking. Applies when the product has AI in it.
3. **Responsible design** - no dark patterns. "No" is a design feature.

## Brand constraint

- Ivory / ink / orange palette
- Geist Sans (body) + Playfair Display (display)
- Simple hyphens, never em dashes
- No gradients, heavy shadow, or decorative glass in the minimal direction

## What the work must feel like

Warm monochrome, air, typographic contrast, flat planes. The default AI palette is forbidden. If every match is warm-minimal editorial, the gate is pointed at the AI default and will certify the default.

## Refusals

- Defense/military work
- German or Russian language requirements
- Dark patterns of any kind
- Synthetic imagery to illustrate my own work (positioning own-goal)
