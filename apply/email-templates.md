# Email outreach templates — Sotiris Iliadis

**Voice rules:** open with an observation about THEM (not your name), 3 short paragraphs, POV ending, spaced hyphen ( - ) never em dash, never "passionate/excited", "13 years of design craft" (never "13 years UX"). Land the AI-native positioning + one proof (Coffee World) + portfolio. Always drop into Gmail **Drafts** — never auto-send.

**When to use email vs the portal:** email for founder-led / small AI startups / "apply by email" roles (skips the anti-bot portal, lands as a human). For big companies with a real ATS portal, use the autofill bookmarklet instead.

## Finding the address (the lane)
- Prefer a **published** address: `careers@` / `jobs@` / `hello@<domain>`, or a named person from their site / LinkedIn.
- Never guess private inboxes or scrape. If there's no real email, use the portal + bookmarklet instead.
- Log every send + a follow-up date (7-10 days) so nothing slips.
