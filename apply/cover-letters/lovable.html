Dear Lovable team,

When the product is an AI that builds interfaces, the design work moves up a level: it's no longer just how a screen looks, it's how a person and a model hand control back and forth - what the AI proposes, what the human keeps, how you stay oriented when the thing is generating your product in front of you. Get that loop right and Lovable feels like magic; get it wrong and it feels like a slot machine.

That loop is exactly what I care about, and I've built it, not just talked about it. I'm a designer with 13 years of craft who ships in code (React, Tailwind, Astro), with AI native to how I work. My clearest proof is public: the Article 50 UI Pattern Kit, an open-source (MIT) React library I designed and built for AI transparency, plus a live demo of the human-in-the-loop moment itself - an AI that discloses what it is, streams its reasoning, shows a real confidence meter, and hands the uncertain calls back to a person to approve, override, or reject (article50-kit.pages.dev). Every component is WCAG 2.1 AA. It's a working answer to the exact question Lovable's product asks: how do you keep a human in control while a model does more of the making.

Lovable is building the tool I'd want a design engineer to obsess over - the interface between human intent and generated software. I bring the rare overlap it needs: real visual craft, code fluency, and a genuinely AI-native workflow, all pointed at trust and clarity. I'd love to help make that loop feel effortless.
